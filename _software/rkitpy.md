My personal Python library for [pandas](https://pandas.pydata.org/) and [polars](https://pola.rs/). It logs what each step of a pipeline changes, like [STATA](https://www.stata.com/) (a [tidylog](https://github.com/elbersb/tidylog) for Python), exports tables to Word, and reports where the rows of a join come from. Featured on [awesome-polars](https://github.com/ddotta/awesome-polars).

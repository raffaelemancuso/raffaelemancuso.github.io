Merge the Word .docx files contained in a folder into a single PDF file.

Insert page numbers, last update date, overlays (e.g. a custom text in every page, such as "work in progress"), and bookmarks into the resulting PDF file.

Useful to put together a PDF of an academic paper from single Word files of title page, paper, tables and figures (the latter exported directly from Python or R).

[Go to the repository](https://github.com/raffaelemancuso/merge-docx)

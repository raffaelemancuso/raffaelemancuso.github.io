My personal R library for empirical research. It exports plots and [modelsummary](https://modelsummary.com/) and [gtsummary](https://www.danieldsjoberg.com/gtsummary/) tables to Word, and adds a 2SLS estimator and alternative optimizers for [glmmTMB](https://github.com/glmmTMB/glmmTMB) models.

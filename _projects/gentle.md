**GeNTLE - Gender Neutrality Tool for Language Exploration**, April 2019 - October 2020.

- University: Polytechnic University of Milan, in collaboration with NTT DATA
- Principal Investigators: Cristina Rossi-Lamastra, Mara Tanelli
- Link: [article in Divercity](https://divercitymag.it/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/DiverCity-10-Linguaggio_compressed-2.pdf#page=20)

During my PhD studies, I helped develop the Python-based software GeNTLE to identify gender biases in corporate texts and business communications through data analytics. This was a collaborative research project between the Polytechnic University of Milan and the multinational consulting company NTT DATA.

## My responsibilities

- Literature review on gendered language
- Write the software in Python
- Test the software on a dataset of New York Times articles and corporate documents (SEC filings)
- Write the documentation of the software

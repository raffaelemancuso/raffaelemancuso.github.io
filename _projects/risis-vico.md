**RISIS 2 - VICO Deepening**, Research Infrastructure for Science and Innovation Policy Studies, April 2019 - February 2023.

- University: Polytechnic University of Milan
- Principal Investigator: Massimiliano Guerini
- Funder: European Union
- Link: [VICO dataset documentation](https://docs.risis.io/datasets/metadata/vico)

I contributed to deepening the VICO dataset, which contains information on start-ups that received venture capital investments from 1998 to 2021, by creating new variables using machine learning models.

## My responsibilities

- Train different machine learning models to classify the firms in the dataset as platform or non-platform
- Test the performance of those models and choose the best one
- Apply the models to the entire dataset

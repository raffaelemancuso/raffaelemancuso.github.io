**RISIS 2 - Cheetah**, Research Infrastructure for Science and Innovation Policy Studies, April 2019 - February 2023.

- University: Polytechnic University of Milan
- Principal Investigator: Massimiliano Guerini
- Funder: European Union
- Link: [Cheetah dataset documentation](https://docs.risis.io/datasets/metadata/cheetah)

RISIS is a European research infrastructure involving 18 partners, including universities and research institutes. I contributed to updating and maintaining the Cheetah database, which contains information on medium-sized firms that experienced rapid growth.

## My responsibilities

- Web-scrape data on fast-growing firms from Orbis
- Data cleaning and data analysis
- Update the database to the new version
- Write the database documentation
- Provide the database in importable format (Stata)

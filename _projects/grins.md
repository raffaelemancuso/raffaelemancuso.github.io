**GRINS - Growing Resilient, Inclusive, and Sustainable**, March 2023 - March 2026.

- University: University of Bologna
- Principal Investigator: Riccardo Fini
- Grant scheme: PNRR (funded by the European Union under the "NextGenerationEU" scheme), Spoke 5, WP 4
- Links: [institutional website](https://grins.it/), [project page at the University of Bologna](https://www.unibo.it/it/ricerca/pnrr-opportunita-aspettative-e-risultati/progetti-pnrr-partenariati-estesi/grins-growing-resilient-inclusive-and-sustainable)

## My responsibilities

- Web-scraping of European Regional Smart Specialization Strategies, both PDF documents and metadata
- Build a retrieval-augmented generation system on the policy PDFs
- Collect and analyze Circular Economy patents from PATSTAT
- Collect and analyze start-ups founded in Europe in the last 10 years from Orbis
- Match DealRoom start-ups on Orbis and download accounting data
- Collect and analyze data on the political ideology of the parties in power
- Write two policy briefs
- Write three scientific papers
- Provide the AMELIA platform with data on the Circular Economy start-ups funded in Europe
